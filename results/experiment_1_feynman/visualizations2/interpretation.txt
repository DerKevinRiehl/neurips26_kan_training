- we ran 4 different models (KAN Bspline, KAN RBF,  MLP Sigmoid, MLP RELU) on Feynman Dataset
- we found that all models performed best by training 1500 iterations given the samples they get
- we found that all models performed best with around 180 parameters, so this is why we compare those
- we measure sample efficiency by NRMSE (normalized root mean square error) over the number of samples given 

- KAN RBF and MLP Sigmoid might not be the best models to compare, as feynman dataset is very heterogeneous,
  and KAN RBF has high std (big variance), and MLP Sigmoid even doesnt learn more given more samples 
  
- Comparison of KAN B-Spline and MLP ReLu shows that KAN-BSpline achieves lower NRMSE than MLP,
	so given the same number of samples it can fit better (given the same number of parameters)
	even on a heterogenoeus dataset such as Feynman
	yet, KAN-B SPLINE has slightly higher variance
