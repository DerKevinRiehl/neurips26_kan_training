Title: KAN: Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks
Citation key used: liu2024kan
Main use in paper: Foundational KAN architecture and motivation for parameter/scaling efficiency.
Summary: The paper introduces KANs as networks with learnable univariate edge functions instead of fixed node activations.
Sample-efficiency angle: It reports strong performance with smaller models and favorable scaling behavior in data fitting and scientific examples.
Caveat: The evidence is closer to parameter efficiency and neural scaling than to a clean fixed-budget sample-efficiency comparison.
