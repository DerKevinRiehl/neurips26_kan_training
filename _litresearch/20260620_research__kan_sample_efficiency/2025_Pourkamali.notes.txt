Title: Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks in Low-Data Regimes: A Comparative Study with Multilayer Perceptrons
Citation key used: pourkamali2025lowdata
Main use in paper: Strong cautionary citation for low-data KAN claims.
Summary: The paper compares KANs with MLPs that use individualized learnable activation functions.
Sample-efficiency angle: In small-data classification settings, the individualized-activation MLPs outperform KANs while using fewer parameters.
Caveat: This suggests KANs should not be assumed to be sample-efficient without careful baselines and matched budgets.
