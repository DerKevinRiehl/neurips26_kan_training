Title: Multifidelity Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks
Citation key used: howard2024multifidelity
Main use in paper: Positive evidence for KANs in expensive-data scientific learning.
Summary: The paper proposes multifidelity KANs, which combine abundant low-fidelity data with limited high-fidelity data to model high-fidelity outputs.
Sample-efficiency angle: It is highly relevant when samples are expensive, because the method reduces reliance on large high-fidelity datasets.
Caveat: This is not an RL study and does not directly compare KAN and MLP sample efficiency under environment-interaction budgets.
